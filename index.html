Sunday meal prep · 60–75 minutes

Aim to prep lunches and make weeknight dinners easier, not cook everything in advance.

Quick lunch portions

Turkey rice bowl

6 oz cooked 93% lean turkey + 1 cup cooked rice + 1–2 cups broccoli + sauce.

~550–650 kcal · 45–50 g protein
Chicken burrito bowl

6 oz cooked chicken + ¾ cup rice + ½ cup beans + peppers, salsa, lettuce and avocado.

~600–700 kcal · 50–60 g protein
Turkey panini

2 slices whole-grain bread + 5–6 oz turkey + cheese, spinach, mustard and apple.

~500–600 kcal · 40–50 g protein

Food safety: refrigerate cooked food promptly, use most refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days, and freeze later portions.
